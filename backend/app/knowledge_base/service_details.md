# Service Details

## Consultation (30 minutes, $25)
A general 30-minute consultation to discuss what you need — whether that's advice on which
service is right for you, a follow-up on a previous visit, or a quick check-in. No preparation
needed. Suitable as a first visit if you're not sure what to book.

## Haircut (45 minutes, $35)
A classic haircut and style, including a consultation on the desired look, wash, cut, and
finish/style. Bring a reference photo if you have a specific style in mind — it helps our
stylists a lot. Walk-ins are not accepted for haircuts; please book in advance.

## Deep Tissue Massage (60 minutes, $80)
A therapeutic massage focused on releasing tension in deeper muscle layers. Recommended for
chronic tension, athletes, or anyone with a physically demanding routine. Please arrive 10
minutes early to fill out a brief health questionnaire on your first visit. Not recommended
during pregnancy without a doctor's note — please mention this when booking so staff can
adjust the treatment.

## Skin Facial (45 minutes, $60)
A rejuvenating facial treatment including cleansing, exfoliation, and a hydrating mask suited to
your skin type. If you have active acne, rosacea, or a known skin allergy, please mention it
when booking so the esthetician can pick suitable products.

## What to bring
For any service, just bring yourself and, if relevant, a reference photo or a list of any
allergies/sensitivities. We don't require any special preparation beyond what's noted above.
