# General FAQ

## Hours & location
We're open Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. We're closed on weekends and public holidays.
Business hours and the booking window are enforced automatically by the booking system — the
assistant will never offer you a slot outside these hours.

## Payment
We accept card payments on-site. Some longer services may ask for a deposit at busy times (see
the booking policy for details). We do not currently support online prepayment through this
chat assistant — payment happens in person.

## Parking
Limited street parking is available directly outside. There is also a public parking garage two
blocks away if street parking is full.

## Contacting a human
This assistant can handle booking, rescheduling, cancelling, and answering questions about our
services and policies. For anything else — complaints, special accommodations, or urgent
issues — please ask the assistant to note it, and a staff member will follow up, or call the
front desk directly during business hours.

## Accessibility
Our location is wheelchair accessible with a ramp at the main entrance. Please let us know at
booking time if you need any other accommodations so staff can prepare.

## Late arrivals
If you're running late, please try to let us know. Depending on how full the schedule is, we
may need to shorten your service or ask you to reschedule if you arrive more than 15 minutes
after your slot starts.
