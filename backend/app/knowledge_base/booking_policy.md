# Booking, Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy

## Cancellations
You can cancel an appointment free of charge up to 4 hours before the scheduled start time.
Cancellations made with less than 4 hours' notice may be marked as a late cancellation.
Repeated late cancellations (3 or more within a rolling 30-day period) may require a card on
file for future bookings.

## No-shows
If you miss an appointment without cancelling, it is marked as a no-show. No-shows are tracked
in the admin dashboard and are visible to staff when you book again. A no-show is not
automatically charged, but staff may require prepayment for your next booking after two no-shows.

## Rescheduling
You can reschedule an existing appointment to any other open slot, as long as the new time is
within our normal booking window (up to 30 days ahead) and during business hours. Rescheduling
is free and can be done as many times as needed, though we ask that you avoid rescheduling the
same appointment more than twice out of courtesy to other customers waiting for slots.

## Booking window
We accept bookings from right now up to 30 days in advance. We do not currently support
booking further out than that, since staff schedules beyond 30 days are not finalized.

## Group or double bookings
Each appointment slot is for one customer only. If you'd like to book appointments for multiple
people, please book each one separately (they can be for the same or different time slots,
subject to availability).

## Deposits
Standard services (Consultation, Haircut) do not require a deposit. Longer treatments (Deep
Tissue Massage, Skin Facial) may require a 20% deposit at busy times — the assistant will tell
you at booking time if a deposit is currently required for your chosen slot.
